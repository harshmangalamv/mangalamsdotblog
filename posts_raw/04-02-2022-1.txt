Title: Present
Date: 2022-02-04

<!-- First published on Blogspot: https://hmangalams.blogspot.com/2022/02/present.html -->

To live in present, is a myth.

The moment sensation is sensed, the event has already passed. There's a miniscule amount of time lag. Processing takes time.

But what we might mean by "living in present" is the tendency to stay closer to the running events with least asymptotic lapse for a longer duration so that the continuum of events happening don't get broken. And we could register most of them into the storage organs in a better organized way.

Additionally, it suggests us to bring our attention pointers to what's happening in front of eyes and in the Era while keeping a note on learned things from past, as apposed to pointing endlessly to events happened (or, to be happening) in backward (or forward) direction of present.
