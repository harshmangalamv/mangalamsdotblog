Title: the hopping monkey
Description: we walk on escalators and weave through traffic to save a few seconds. a monkey has no such urgency. does any of it matter?

there are people who walk on a running escalator. just in to reach somewhere quicker. they might be returning after a tiring day at the work, or maybe running late for the next bus to home at the airport.

there are those waiting for the endless traffic to turn green.

among many commonalities, we can agree that they are trying to cut the distance and make it a short.

one can choose to not walk on the escalator. and even if doing so saved 30 seconds, what did it result? what worse could happen if they miss the bus? next one is always in the line. well, waiting for the traffic has got no solution. no one wants to be stuck in the traffic forever except when there is disliking to the place they are arriving to. but if you are on a two-wheeler you can nudge your way faster by changing lanes like a hopping monkey.

well, apparently the monkey has got none of these. they don't have this sense of urgency. they can hop in any direction they want provided they are not in yet another similarly optimized structure as the humans do.

there is a tradeoff. clearly. the prime question is if all these matter at all? do they need to?
