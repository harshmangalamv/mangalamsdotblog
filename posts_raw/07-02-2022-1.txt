Title: Free Trial
Date: 2022-02-07

Free trials in every corner.

There are premium services that are promised for a specific period, so that you could know more about them. Plenty of them.

This costs your time and energy. But without doing that it's not possible to know the service too. Yet there are reviews - some genuine some manipulated. So there is a little help to it.

And hence you have an option to step back.

But not everything comes with free trial. Things of accountability and newness being in the group. That's where instincts, experience and basics are tested. You win it or get slapped.

And to maximize the chances of success there, you need to expand your mental horizons and experience. There will be setbacks, things won't go the way desired. But there's no other way. You learn regardless of everything. Step by step. Each day. Every minute. Just to become better at things that don't come with a free trial.
