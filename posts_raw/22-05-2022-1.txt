Title: Games
Date: 2022-05-22

<!-- First published on Blogspot: https://hmangalams.blogspot.com/2022/05/games.html -->

Of almost all the games human device, there is a level minimum life points and some certain predefined parameters. Irrespective of the player. You lose the game once you fail to satisfy a criteria. You win and perhaps can have a couple of bonus points once you manage to gain a minimum cutoff. All in all it's certainly a fare share. You have the way, you're lucky enough.

Life is also a long term game. Non undo-able. You don't necessarily have a bare minimum to start with. You might have even the start itself on a loan. You don't start a level once you feel confident enough of an easier level, but you are slapped with that regardless of your familiarity let alone of being good at it. If it hits you, it's you who can save you. Everyone is busy. No colleagues on helpline. You survive even someone facing the same level would have already given up or, you find someone else doing better at the same level where you're clueless. There is no defined bare minimum to have in order to be in the game. It's subjective. One can reach a level and can hands up while someone would find it better to have a higher level as their finish line. No minimum criteria defined for the win statement too. Again, it's subjective. It's unfair, unlike those simulated human-made ones. It matters where you have come from, who you are, what goes in your mind, how you look at the problem statement and what you are doing. It also matters that you continue doing it, reiterating-correcting and be conscious.

The two games are different. The same subject looks much different in those two circumstances. But the latter one is long lived and real. It is the one that matters more. The only one the human never opted for, but got that out of blue. And since it is, you have to play whatever comes the way. Period.
