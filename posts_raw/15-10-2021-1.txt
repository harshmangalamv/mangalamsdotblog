Title: Conflict
Date: 2021-10-15

<!-- First published on Blogspot: https://hmangalams.blogspot.com/2021/10/conflict.html -->

It's experienced when two or more desires or motives of same intensity exist at the same time and we've to choose one among them. It's a tough situation. Primarily it's of three kinds viz intrapersonal, interpersonal and unconscious.

While last two are not in one's control, the first one can be controlled to some extent. Since it occurs within the thinking systems, observing how and why things occur inside it are helpful for this case.

Realizing that any of the choices that one choses are based on limited inputs experienced. And, since uncertainties are one of the aspects that often change the scene. The outcome hence observed will also be the one with limited consequences. Hardly there was any role of imagination involved from the decider's end, but fear.

The only possible way out is to let the things the way they are going, and trying simultaneously with no desire for the specific kind of result from the choice made. But this is better said, than done.

As human, we tend to be afraid of uncertainties and hence are driven largely by fear. Our every fear finds its root in our desire. We are afraid of any consequences other than those defined by our desires.

So, in spite of that, we can think this way: removing the sense of identity with certain predefined things and considering every living creature as same biological entity, and realizing the fundamental functioning of them. And, then allowing our conscience to see things exactly as they are, rather than looking to them from the view of what they should be according to our desires. We must have to face our very own self and it's never easy. Without it, we are not aware of what exactly is going inside.
