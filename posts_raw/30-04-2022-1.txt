Title: Moon
Date: 2022-04-30

<!-- First published on Blogspot: https://hmangalams.blogspot.com/2022/04/moon.html -->

Man aspired the moon. It still aspires. They treat it as their symbol that they love, a journey that they want to sail and a heavenly unearthly entity to look for in times of testing.

Moon is seen highly and treated in all highness. Its significance in literatures is widespread. Moon is mystic. A filler for the void that embraces humanity. Perhaps a companion in short times of realization of experiencing a part of vast aloneness that occupies every systems, when man finds themselves in rare unsharable inexpressible moments of critical existence. Moon doesn't tell the answer. But it is the only companion man finds in those times, regardless of their age, position or anything.

But moon itself has phases. Full moon is not the moon man should only think of. There are phases too. Some days with no presence, some other partly visible. Moon has their own story. Ups and downs are also there. Without their acknowledgement, the man's image of moon is partial and deceiving. A moon is moon not because of its full phase but also because of what goes beyond and beneath it.
