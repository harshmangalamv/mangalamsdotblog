You are sold things that are advertised to be of much importance. You are made sure they appear really important. But in reality seldom they are.

This highly structured and curated program imposed unto you has ambiguous consequences that are subject to debate. While medicines with no proper clinical trial and certifications are held illegal, this programming does not abide any structured rule. Yet it is acceptable.

With thing as such, only precautions will help. There is need of balance between the virtual and the real.
