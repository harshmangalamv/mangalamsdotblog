The easiest and repetitive tasks are the hardest to do.

Sleeping at right time, coming out of bed early, eating at the right time. And writing this blog everyday, by taking some minutes from a day. Completing a small task or anything, everything can be broken into smaller pieces, easier ones. But we fail.

Nothing more to say. All is self explainable - we tend to be on auto run. The mechanism of which the software is so old as anything - that living ways has got changed, but what that is floating in it is still running on an older infrastructure.

Human is a fool because it is easily manipulatable - different medias are doing it i.e. what you consume digitally being the major one - that it fools its carrier (the one who carries), the us.

Additionally those silly realizations of enough time still remaining. Perhaps nothing worse can happen more than this.

But the good news is that the software can be updated, brain can be fooled by the carrier, and the chaotic uncertainty of life can be realized and lived. This is pretty easy. But hard! Until done?
