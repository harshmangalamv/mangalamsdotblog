You're not heard until you explode.

The negative explosion marks a chaotic kink followed by a highly probable instantaneous drop to an infinitely constant zero. The positive explosion initiates exponentiation and a new potential well of whose height remains undefined for positive.

No explosion, no sound; but you can always explode for positive.
